Privacy Policy and Data Deletion

Last updated: September 2026

1. What Data We Collect

SchoolBot only processes: the parent's WhatsApp phone number, the text of incoming messages, and school data entered in Google Sheets.

2. How We Use Data

Data is used solely to respond to parent enquiries and send school announcements. We do not sell any data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Parent conversation content is not stored or logged. School data is stored in Google Sheets under full control of school management.

4. Third-Party Services

We use: WhatsApp Business API (Meta) for messaging, Google Sheets API for school data, and Railway.app for hosting.
