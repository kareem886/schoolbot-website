Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

Last updated: September 2026

1. What Data We Collect

SmarvexAI solutions only process: the user's phone number or contact identifier, the text of incoming messages, and business data entered by the client in their management system (e.g. Google Sheets).

2. How We Use Data

Data is used solely to respond to user enquiries and deliver the automation service. We do not sell any data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Conversation content is not stored or logged beyond the session. Business data is stored under full control of the client.

4. Third-Party Services

Our solutions may use: WhatsApp Business API (Meta), Google Sheets API, Railway.app for hosting, and the Anthropic/OpenAI API for AI capabilities.

5. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data contact us via email: kareem@smarvexai.com. All requests processed within 48 hours.
